# IO, second implementation: plan

`18-io.md` stays the design. This plan rewrites its implementation as
`src/io2/`, then swaps it in for `src/io/` and deletes the old module.
No code in `src/io/` is carried over except the outward-facing shapes
listed under "Kept".

## Why

`src/io/` is about 7,100 lines outside its tests, plus about 1,000 more in
`wasm/`, `pack/`, `dl/`, `fn_system/`, and the channel adapter. Most of
it comes from five places:

- **Three token spaces.** A system token (slot and generation) is mapped
  to a reactor token from a slab, with a second slab of reactor-own
  tokens (`OWN`), an `UNTRACKED` token for cancels and closes, and a
  `route` field for sim ops. Each layer allocates and translates.
- **Two handle tables.** `Handles` maps a system handle to an `Os`
  handle, and each `Os` keeps its own slab of slots with generations,
  dial targets, and `register`.
- **A directory inside the arena.** `BufTable` keeps a little-endian
  directory in its memory so a guest and the host can share it. That
  brings three arena kinds, `RefCell`, a re-check of every entry on every
  read, `Corrupt`, `export`, `refill`, `release_all`, and `rewind`.
- **Sim routing through the reactor.** `lower_routed`, `unroute`, `raise`,
  `sim_cancels`, and `requeue_sim_cancel` exist only so sim ops share the
  reactor's tokens.
- **Shared state behind `RefCell`.** `IoShared` holds tables, members,
  resources, grants, and an optional host. `Member` holds a `Quiesce` of
  three `Cell`s. The queue lends its state through `Cell<Option<Box<_>>>`.

## The new shape

The core idea: **every index is arithmetic over tables sized at build,
and a buffer is a span, not a slot.** Nothing is translated through a map,
and nothing allocates after build.

### Wire types (core, wasm-capable)

- **`Span { off: u32, len: u32 }`** names bytes in the submitting
  system's arena. For a read, `len` is the capacity. For a write, it is
  the filled length.
- **`Sqe`** is a flat `#[repr(C)]` struct, like io_uring's: `token`,
  `handle`, `opcode: u8`, `buf: Span`, `offset: u64`, and an `arg` of
  bytes for the few ops that carry more (tty config, GPIO value, open
  flags, a send address). It is `FromBytes`. An unknown opcode is
  checked once, where `io_submit` lowers the op, and fails with
  `Unsupported`. There are no hand-padded enum variants.
- **`Cqe { token, status, value }`** is 12 bytes. `value` is a length or
  a handle.
- **`Token`** is `slot | generation << 16`, with `slot < MAX_OPS`.
  **`Handle`** is `index | generation << 16` into the system's handle
  table.
- `IoStatus`, `IoError`, and `Errno` keep their codes and names.

### The system side (core)

- **`Arena`**: one per system. Its constructor bump-allocates into a
  `Vec<u8>`. It freezes when the make ends, and a remake rewinds it to the
  start and must fit. It is backed by owned memory, a dylib's region under
  a lease, or a wasm mirror. It has no directory.
- **`Buf { arena: Rc<Arena>, off, cap, len }`**: the length lives in the
  `Buf`, not in the arena. Dropping a `Buf` does nothing. Its bytes come
  back on the next remake, as they do today.
- **`Io`**: the system's queue. It holds the SQ output, the CQ input, and
  `[Cell<OpState>; MAX_OPS]`, where `OpState` is
  `Free | Waiting | Orphan | Done { status, value }`. That is `Copy`, so
  no state is lent and nothing can be reentered.
- **`Pending<O>`** holds its token and its `Buf`. A completion sets the
  `Buf`'s length. When a `Pending` drops, its slot becomes `Orphan`. An
  orphaned op that opened a handle is closed under its own token, which
  frees the slot when the close lands. There is no untracked token.
- **`Pending::cancel`** writes a `Cancel` op that names its target's
  token. A cancel takes no slot and never gets a completion.
- `Handle<K>`, `Cap<K>`, `Slot<O>`, `IoInit`, and the `Interface`
  markers keep their public API.

### The host side (`host`, unix)

- **`Dispatch`** is a plain struct with no IO, owned by the io steps:
  - `members: Box<[Member]>`: each member's arena base and length, its
    handle table (its grants, then `MAX_OPS` derived slots), and a
    `quiescing` flag;
  - `ops: Box<[OpEntry]>`, `members × MAX_OPS` long. The op that member
    `m` submits in slot `s` is entry `m * MAX_OPS + s`. Each entry holds
    its generation, resource, kind, and whether a cancel was sent;
  - `resources: Box<[Resource]>`: each one's backing, `Target` (an owned
    fd, or the sim port index), counters, and name.

  It has two halves: `lower(m, sqe) -> Route | Cqe`, where `Route` is
  `Os(OsOp)` or `Sim(res, Sqe)`, and `raise(OsDone) -> (m, Cqe)`.
  Quiescing is `begin(m)`, `idle(m)`, and `settle(m)`, which closes
  derived handles and rewinds the arena. All of it is unit-testable, and
  Kani can prove its index arithmetic.
- **`Os`** is a trait with two implementations at once (a real backend
  and a fake for tests), so the trait is justified. It takes ops already
  resolved:
  `OsOp { id, target: RawFd | Fixed, op, buf: Option<NonNull<[u8]>>, arena }`.
  Its methods are `push`, `cancel(id)`, `submit`,
  `reap(&mut Vec<OsDone>)`, and `close(target)`. `close` runs
  synchronously, so the reactor sends no ops of its own. The `Os` keeps
  no handle table and has no `register`.
- **`UringOs`** registers each member's arena as fixed buffer `m`.
  Derived sockets go in its sparse fixed-file table. **`PollOs`** parks
  ops that hit `EAGAIN` and polls for readiness.
- **The io steps.** `io_submit` drains each SQ, calls `lower`, and either
  pushes the op to the `Os` or writes it to `{res}.req`. `io_complete`
  reaps the `Os`, drains `{res}.rep`, calls `raise`, writes the CQs, and
  writes `io_status`. A sim op uses the same op entry as a real one,
  which removes the routing API.
- **Resources**: `open(RealKind) -> OwnedFd` at build, with termios, CAN,
  GPIO, UDP, file, dir, and listener setup. `Dispatch` owns the
  descriptors.

### Sim

- `DeviceSide<K>` reads `{res}.req` and writes `{res}.rep`. Each request
  carries a member index and a `Span`, which `DeviceSide` resolves through
  an `Rc<[ArenaRef]>`. It keeps `Held` (ops that are held or have
  latency) and an outbox.
- `Model<K>`, `UartModel`, `GpioModel`, and `Reply` keep their public API.

### Guests

- A region is an arena's base and length. The pack's ctor bump-allocates
  into it, and the host reads it under a lease (dylib) or keeps a copy
  (wasm).
- For wasm, `IoCopy` copies each submitted write's span into the mirror
  and each finished read's span back out. Every `Sqe` carries its span, so
  nothing is looked up.
- Because the region has no directory, this is **ABI 11**.

## Kept

These shapes face outward: config, Python, the panel, golden files, and
packs. They move into `io2` unchanged:

- `ResourceConfig`, `Backing`, `RealKind`, `SimKind`, `CanFilter`,
  `GpioDirection`, `Access` (`kind.rs`);
- `Grant`, `GrantDef`, `End`, `IO_SQ`, `IO_CQ`, `MAX_OPS`, and the
  `io_submit`/`io_complete` type names;
- the `ResourceStatus` frame;
- the public names `Cap`, `Handle`, `Buf`, `Slot`, `Pending`, `IoInit`,
  `DeviceSide`, `Io`, `Interface`, and the interface markers;
- `Rng`, which leaves `io` for the crate root, since it is not IO.

Outside `src/io/`, the Python builder, `CoordinatorConfig`, `SystemConfig`,
the build checks in `coordinator/build.rs`, the channel adapter's
`Quiescing` state, and stellarator's `turn` and `adopt` all stay. Their
call sites are rewired in phase 5.

## Phases

`io2` is built beside `io` and wired to nothing until phase 5. Each phase
ends with its tests passing and is committed on its own.

| Phase | What | Exit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wire types, `Arena`/`Buf`/`IoInit`, `Io`/`Pending`/`Slot`, `Handle`/`Cap` | Unit tests over an in-memory SQ/CQ loopback: submit, complete, orphan, cancel, `QueueFull`, stale handle, remake rewind. |
| 2 | `Dispatch` | Unit tests against a fake `Os`: lowering, raising, derived handles, close, quiesce, counters. Kani: the op index and handle generation never alias. |
| 3 | `Os`, `PollOs`, `UringOs`, resource opening | `tests/os_cases` rewritten for the new trait, run under `io_poll` and `io_uring` (Linux VM). |
| 4 | Sim: `DeviceSide`, `Held`, models, `io2::test` harness | The `sim/tests` behaviors carried over: latency, a held read, cancel while held, a full reply port. |
| 5 | Swap | io steps over `Dispatch`; rewire `fn_system` bind and ctor, `MakeCx.io`, the channel adapter's quiesce, `pack`/`dl`/`wasm`, `link`'s registration, and stubgen. Delete `src/io/` and rename `io2` to `io`. ABI 11. `tests/io.rs`, `sim.rs`, `dl.rs`, `determinism.rs`, `wasm.rs`, `slot.rs`, and the echo-pack GPS pass with only mechanical edits. |
| 6 | Antagonistic review, then fixes | Review by another model. |

**Line budget:** `io2` at most 3,500 lines outside its tests, against
about 7,100 today. Over budget is a signal to stop and ask.

## Decisions

1. **Spans in `Sqe`.** Yes. The region has no directory, and the ABI
   goes to 11.
2. **Dial, `Resolve`, and `Connect`** are left out of `io2`.
   `RealKind::TcpConnect` stays in config, and build refuses it until
   links land. `Cqe` carries no `RawAddr`.
3. **One swap commit.** `io2` stays unwired until phase 5. Breaking
   changes to internal or pack-facing APIs are allowed when they make
   the swap quicker. The public names are kept where that costs nothing.
